What it does

Bothive gives each Bot its own chat, memory and folder. You start with one, Hive, and add more when you want them.

Works with the AI you have

Bots run through an AI command-line tool already on your computer (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build or OpenCode) or a local model with Ollama or LM Studio. You keep your own account and settings.

Talk from anywhere

Your iPhone connects to your computer with end-to-end encryption. Start a voice call with a Bot and keep it going from the Dynamic Island while you use other apps.

Connects to your apps

Add a Composio key to let Bots read and act in Gmail, Calendar, Notion, GitHub and more. Anything that sends, creates, changes or deletes asks you first.

Free and open source

No subscription and no ads. You only pay for the AI accounts you already use. The code is MIT-licensed on GitHub.

Your data stays yours

Bots, chats, memory and logins stay on your own computer. The free account only connects your iPhone, and messages between your devices are encrypted end to end and deleted within 90 seconds. No analytics, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Opening it the first time

Until the apps are signed by a registered developer, your computer will ask whether to trust them. Download only from this site or the project's GitHub Releases.

Mac

  1. Open the DMG and drag Bothive to Applications.
  2. Open Bothive. macOS says it can't check it for malware. Choose Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway next to Bothive.
  4. Open Bothive again and confirm. You only do this once.

Windows

  1. Run the Setup file.
  2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the installer. Bothive opens when it finishes.

Check the download (optional)

Each Mac release comes with a .sha256 file. In Terminal, in your Downloads folder:

shasum -a 256 -c Bothive-*-mac-universal.dmg.sha256

It should print OK.
